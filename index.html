📜 The Five Demands

The Manifesto.

Read it once. Read it twice. Then send it to someone who needs to read it.

01
Judicial Independence

If the CKP comes in power, no Chief Justice shall be granted a Rajya Sabha seat as a post-retirement reward. Justice must not be auctioned for political loyalty.

⚖️
02
Electoral Integrity

If any legitimate vote is deleted — whether in a CKP or opposition-ruled state — the Chief Election Commissioner shall be arrested under UAPA. Taking away a citizen's voting rights is no less than terrorism.

🗳️
03
True Gender Representation

Women shall receive 50% reservation — not 33% — without increasing the strength of Parliament. Additionally, 50% of all Cabinet positions shall be reserved for women. Tokenism ends here.

✊
04
Media Freedom

All media houses owned by Ambani and Adani shall have their licences cancelled to make way for truly independent media. Bank accounts of Godi media anchors shall be investigated. The news is not a press release.

📺
05
Anti-Defection Zero Tolerance

Any MLA or MP who defects from one party to another shall be barred from contesting elections — and from holding any public office — for 20 years. Loyalty to the people, not to the highest bidder.
